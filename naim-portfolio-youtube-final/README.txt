NAIM PERSONAL PORTFOLIO — UPDATED VERSION

1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in your browser.
3. Your profile photo is already included in assets/images/profile.png.

ADDING PROJECT VIDEOS
---------------------
Put your MP4 videos in assets/videos/ with these exact names:

Greenhouse project:
assets/videos/greenhouse.mp4

Surveillance robot:
assets/videos/surveillance-robot.mp4

Digital logic:
assets/videos/digital-logic.mp4

The website automatically hides the empty video player when a video is missing.

EDITING YOUR INFORMATION
------------------------
Open index.html in Notepad or VS Code and replace the placeholder university,
HSC, SSC, email, LinkedIn, and GitHub information with your own details.
